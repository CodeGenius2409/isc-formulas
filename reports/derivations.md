# Derivations

The derivations and proofs the 2028 syllabus expects, from its "derive" lines and the paper analysis. Generated by `tests/unit/derivation-report-private.test.ts`. This holds counts only, and no wording from any paper.

**118 derivations.** Each has a bank question, so it can be asked at 0% generated, and blueprints that ask it in the other ways the papers do: from a stated start, with its assumptions, with a step missing, with a mistake to find where one was written, and with "hence calculate" where one fits.

"In the papers" counts the analysed question parts whose wording matches it. It's a guide, as a short or OCR-damaged part can be missed, and a broad match can overcount.

"Checked" means both sides of the result were evaluated by mathjs at random values and agreed (`tests/unit/derivations.test.ts`), and that their units matched where the expression allows it. **97 of 118** are checked like this.
